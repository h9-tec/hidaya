# هداية الحيارى إلى اللسانيات الحاسوبية بجدارة

A free, gated path from first principles to computational linguistics, in one static page.

- 144 lessons across 7 stations: foundations, machine learning, deep learning, vision and reinforcement learning, NLP, computational linguistics and LLMs, research and engineering.
- Each lesson plays the original course inside the page (official YouTube lecture playlists and course pages, embedded from their own sources) and ends with a short two-question check. The next lesson unlocks only when the check is passed.
- Progress is saved in your browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Arabic and English side by side. Works on phones.

All videos, course pages and books belong to their authors and institutions. A few publishers do not allow embedding; those lessons link to the source instead. The short checks are original.
